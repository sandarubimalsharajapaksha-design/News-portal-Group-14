About the University of Ruhuna

The University of Ruhuna is a leading public university in Sri Lanka, located in the beautiful Southern Province. Established in 1978, the university has become an important centre for higher education, research, and academic excellence in the country. The university is committed to providing quality education and developing knowledgeable, skilled, and responsible graduates who can contribute positively to society. It offers a wide range of academic programmes across several disciplines, including Science, Management, Humanities and Social Sciences, Medicine, Agriculture, Engineering, and Fisheries and Marine Sciences.With a strong focus on education, research, innovation, and student development, the University of Ruhuna continues to create opportunities for students to achieve their academic and professional goals.The university also encourages extracurricular activities, cultural events, sports, and community engagement, helping students develop leadership skills, creativity, and teamwork.
