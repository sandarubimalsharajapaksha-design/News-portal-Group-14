About Our News Portal

Welcome to the University of Ruhuna News Portal. This platform is designed to keep students, lecturers and staff informed about the latest university news, announcements and important updates. Our goal is to provide a reliable and convenient way to access university-related information in one place.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide accurate and timely university information while keeping students and staff connected with important academic and campus activities.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted digital information platform that connects the University of Ruhuna community through accessible and reliable news.

About the University of Ruhuna

The University of Ruhuna is a public university in Sri Lanka. It provides higher education opportunities across various academic disciplines. Our news portal aims to help its university community stay informed about important activities and developments.
